feat: serve MCP revision 2026-07-28 over stdio alongside 2025-11-25 (Story 43.2)

`McpServerBase.start()` serves stdio through the SDK's `serveStdio`, so one process answers a 2025-11-25 `initialize` and a 2026-07-28 `server/discover`. The 2025-11-25 wire is unchanged (every Story 43.1 golden line and pin passes as it was), and the suite stays at 115 tools with the governance baseline and `BOOTSTRAP_VERSION` unmoved. The 2026-07-28 wire is the same content plus the fields that revision mandates (`resultType` and the server identity in `_meta` on every result, `ttlMs` and `cacheScope` on the cacheable methods), without the tools' `execution`. A per-request `_meta` key changes no `server` or namespace resolution, governance decision, visibility or audit entry.

Claude Code 2.1.292 and later negotiate 2026-07-28 with stdio servers by default and fell back to 2025-11-25 against earlier servers. `MCP_PROTOCOL_NEGOTIATION=legacy` in Claude Code's own environment keeps the 2025-11-25 handshake. Known limits, all the SDK's: on 2026-07-28 a `ping` is answered `-32601`, `list_changed` reaches only `subscriptions/listen` subscribers, and a v2 SDK client on `auto` or a pinned revision starts a short-lived probe process per connect.
