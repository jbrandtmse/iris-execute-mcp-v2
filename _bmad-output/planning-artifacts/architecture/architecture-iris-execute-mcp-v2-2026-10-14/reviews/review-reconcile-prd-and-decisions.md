# Reconciliation review: spine vs PRD and Lead decisions

## Verdict

The spine adopts the main points of S-2, S-3, L-2, L-3, L-4 and D1–D7 correctly. Several settled constraints did not make it in:

- the shared-IRIS-instance rule;
- most of the licensing conditions;
- the closed list of sanctioned behavior changes.

Two cross-story seams are neither decided nor deferred: the IPM-versus-bootstrap version marker (FR158 × FR159), and the PHI policy's scope against the new `iris_sql_query`.

**Counts:** 5 high, 13 medium, 7 low. Tallied mechanically from the Severity column below (Rule #51).

## Inputs that landed correctly (spot-checked)

- **S-2 → AD-23:** stdio only, rejection in `resolveTransport`, Streamable HTTP deferred with an auth, bind-address and session design.
- **S-3 → AD-4 and AD-24:** a `defaultEnabled` write, blocked by `read-only` (AD-6), outside the baseline (AD-5). The closed-list gap is R8.
- **L-2 → AD-26:** default `redact`; a body the engine cannot redact is blocked; the resend preview is in scope. The gaps are R4, R10 and R11.
- **L-3 → AD-25:** the read-classified `iris_sql_query`, never a weakened write key. The cap gap is R7.
- **L-4 → AD-7:** `core` as a guideline. The gap is R19.
- **D1–D4, D7 → AD-29 and Stack:** the fixed group at 1.0.0, `@1`, `engines >=22`, the Node 22/24/26 floors, secret-free CI on throwaway containers, gated publishing with provenance.
- **D6 → AD-14:** the class half lands; the version half is R5. **D5:** only the non-endorsement half is missing (R2).
- **FR127–FR145:** covered by AD-3 to AD-11 and AD-16 to AD-21, except the client-config write protocol (R12) and annotations (R21).
- **S-1, S-4 and L-1** (tracker reconciliation, batch mode, wave branches) are process decisions recorded in epics.md and branch-naming.yaml. Leaving them out of the spine is correct.
- **D1's fallback scope** (a personal scope if the `iris-mcp` organization is unavailable) is an Epic 42 risk with a "confirm before 42.1" action. The spine can stay silent unless the fallback fires.
